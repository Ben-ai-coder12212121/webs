Game art used by Detourr's 3D games
- Textures, HDRIs and props: Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0.
- Leaf-cluster foliage textures: ambientCG (ambientcg.com), CC0.
- Characters: three.js example models (Soldier, Xbot, Michelle from Mixamo; Ready Player Me sample avatar).
- Loaders: three.js r128 examples (MIT).
- Animated characters (chars/q_*.glb): Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0: Universal Base Characters (head, eyes, hair),
  Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Ranger and Peasant outfits) and Universal Animation Library (motion clips).
  Converted for the web: textures downsized to WebP, cloth mask baked into the outfit texture's alpha channel.
- Trees, rocks, grass and bushes (models/n_nature.glb): Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit, CC0. Leaf textures greyed so games can tint them.
