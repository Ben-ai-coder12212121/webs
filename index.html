About Detourr

Detourr is a free collection of browser games, puzzles, sports challenges, music toys and time-killers. No account needed and no ads.

Privacy

Detourr doesn’t ask for personal information. High scores, difficulty settings and streaks live in your own browser’s storage on this device. Clearing your browser data erases them.

Leaderboards: if you choose a leaderboard name, that nickname and your best scores in skill games are sent to our server and shown publicly. Use a nickname, not your real name. Analytics: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which games are popular.

Fair play

Detourr is an independent project. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any other game website, app, company or brand. Every game here is original code written for Detourr. Classic game ideas like tic-tac-toe, sudoku and mazes are public-domain traditions, and names of people, brands and games in Hype Check are only there to identify quiz topics.
