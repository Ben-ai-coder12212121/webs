About Detour

Detour is a free collection of browser games, puzzles, sports challenges, music toys and time-killers. No account, no ads, and no tracking.

Privacy

Detour doesn’t collect personal information. High scores, difficulty settings and streaks live only in your own browser’s storage on this device. Clearing your browser data erases them.

Fair play

Detour is an independent project. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any other game website, app, company or brand. Every game here is original code written for Detour. Classic game ideas like tic-tac-toe, sudoku and mazes are public-domain traditions, and names of people, brands and games in Hype Check are only there to identify quiz topics.
