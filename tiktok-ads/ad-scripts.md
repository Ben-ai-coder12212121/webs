# Detour: TikTok ad pack

Six short ads, each 15–25 seconds. Each one has a matching cover image (`ad1.png` to `ad6.png`, 1080×1920). Use the cover as the opening frame or the video's thumbnail.

**Before you post**
- Put your site link in your TikTok bio. Links in captions aren't clickable in normal posts, so every ad says "link in bio".
- Record the gameplay yourself with your phone's screen recorder, or OBS on a computer. Tap **⛶ Full screen** in the game first so the footage fills the frame.
- Use a trending sound at low volume under the game audio. TikTok favours posts that use trending sounds.
- Keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right edge of the screen, where TikTok's buttons and caption sit. The covers already do this.
- Post one ad a day for a week, then keep making more of whichever style gets the most views.

## Posting tips
- **Hooks matter most.** If a post gets few views, re-post the same gameplay with a different first 2 seconds.
- **Reply to comments with videos.** For example, if someone asks "which villain is best?", answer with a clip of that villain.
- **Cross-post** the same videos to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
- **Paid ads (TikTok Promote or Ads Manager):** these need a website link, so point them at your real domain once the site is live. Keep the targeting broad at first.
- **Rules:**
  - Don't claim to be affiliated with other games or brands, like "Papa's Pizzeria".
  - Don't use copyrighted music outside TikTok's own sound library.
  - Anything aimed at under-13s isn't allowed on TikTok.
