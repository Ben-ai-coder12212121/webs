Getting it right in Pennsylvania since 2003.

Corl Communications, Inc. is a structured cabling, fiber optic, and low-voltage contractor headquartered at 1601 South 19th Street, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania 17104, serving central Pennsylvania and statewide since 2003. The company is owned by William (Bill) Corl, RCDD, a BICSI Prestige Platinum member and retired Pennsylvania Air National Guard Senior Master Sergeant. Corl is a Hubbell Certified Installer and holds a Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Master IT Services ITQ contract for cabling services. Services: Category 6 and 6A structured cabling, single-mode and multimode fiber, outside plant underground and aerial, Ekahau wireless surveys and access point installation, multi-site rollouts, security and nurse-call cabling, AV rooms, RCDD design, and moves, adds, and changes. Our story

Corl Communications, Inc. has designed and installed telecommunications infrastructure from 1601 South 19th Street in Harrisburg since 2003. We started as a small crew pulling copper and terminating fiber for local businesses. Today our technicians install and certify structured cabling, fiber optic, and low-voltage systems in hospitals, schools, state and federal facilities, banks, and manufacturing plants across Pennsylvania.

What has not changed is how we work. Every job is designed or reviewed by a Registered Communications Distribution Designer, installed by our own employees rather than day labor, tested to the manufacturer's warranty requirements, and handed over with the documentation the owner needs to run the building. That is why general contractors bring us back and why facility managers call us directly when a building changes.

About Bill Corl

Bill Corl is the President and owner of Corl Communications. He has held the RCDD credential since 2005 and holds BICSI Prestige Platinum status.

Alongside running Corl, Bill served 36 years in the Pennsylvania Air National Guard, retiring in 2026 as a Senior Master Sergeant with the 211th Engineering Installation Squadron, a unit that engineers and installs communications infrastructure for the Air Force. The discipline of that work, where a system is either installed to the standard and documented or it is not accepted, is the discipline Corl brings to every commercial job.
