Structured Cabling

Category 6 and 6A horizontal cabling, copper backbone, and voice-grade cabling for new construction, renovations, and tenant fit-outs. We install to whatever the spec names, Hubbell (as a certified installer), Panduit, Leviton and Berk-Tek, Corning, Belden, or CommScope, label to your standard, test every link, firestop our own sleeves and penetrations, and register the manufacturer's 20- to 25-year warranty in the owner's name. Division 27 submittals, redline as-builts, and the closeout package come with the job.

Single-mode and multimode fiber for backbones, campus links, and fiber-to-the-desk. Installation, termination, splicing, repair, and certified loss and OTDR testing with documented results. Corning is our standard fiber platform; we install to your spec when the owner has one.

Outside Plant

Cable between buildings and across campuses, underground and aerial.

Underground: conduit and duct systems, directional boring and open trench through our excavation partners, PA One Call locates, direct-bury and armored fiber, building entrance protection and grounding.

Aerial: fiber and copper placed on utility poles, strand-and-lash or self-supporting (ADSS) fiber, pole-attachment and make-ready coordination with the utility, and the drop into the building.

We price and build single-mode links from 12 to 96 strands, from a few hundred feet to campus scale.

Wireless Surveys & Access Point Installation

Ekahau site surveys before and after installation: a predictive design from your floor plans, a pre-installation survey that maps coverage, interference, and access point placement, and a post-installation validation survey with heat maps that prove the network performs where people actually work.

We install the Category 6A drops, mount the access points, label and document every AP by location and MAC address, and hand the network team a report they can keep. We also run AP refresh programs, swapping end-of-life access points and verifying the cabling behind them, at one office or across a whole portfolio.
