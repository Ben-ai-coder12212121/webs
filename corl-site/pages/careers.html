Learn a trade that is in every building.

Every hospital, school, office, and plant runs on the cabling behind its walls, and somebody has to install it right. At Corl Communications you learn that trade from technicians who have done it for decades, in our own training lab in Harrisburg and on real job sites across Pennsylvania.

No experience is required to start. A driver's license, a clean background for hospital and school sites, and the willingness to show up and learn are.

Apply

The path

Apprentice technician

Year one: pulling and terminating copper, learning pathways, labeling, and testing in the lab and in the field beside a lead tech.

Technician

Years two and three: fiber termination and splicing, telecom room build-outs, camera and access-control device installation, and running your own small jobs.

Lead technician / foreman

Running crews, reading prints, closing out projects, and OSHA 30.

Our structured three-year program follows the Pennsylvania registered-apprenticeship model, roughly 6,000 hours of on-the-job training plus classroom time in our lab.

What you get

  • Paid training from day one, including OSHA 10 and manufacturer certification courses.
  • Group medical and vision coverage.
  • 401(k) retirement plan.
  • Company vehicle or mileage for job-site travel, tools, and PPE provided.
  • Work in your own state, home most nights.
