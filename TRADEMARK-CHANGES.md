## Kept on purpose: please review

These mention real brands or works, but only as facts or quiz answers. They don't claim a link to the brand. Tell me if you want any of them changed too.

- **Birthday Time Machine, How Old Is It?, Earth Right Now:** dated facts such as “Super Mario Bros. was released (1985)”, “Google was founded”, “Minecraft came out”, and live counters for Google searches.
- **Hype Check:** a “which is more searched” game that compares names like Fortnite, Roblox, Minecraft and Taylor Swift, with credited Wikimedia photos.
- **Guess the Song, Beat Lanes, Tap Tiles, Learn Piano:** pop songs by title and artist (Sabrina Carpenter, Billie Eilish, Michael Jackson, a-ha…), played as very short simplified snippets. The franchise film, TV and game themes are removed, and the rest are public-domain classical and folk pieces. Pop-song snippets are a copyright question rather than a trademark one. If you want zero risk there, they should go too.
- **Where Am I?:** says it uses “real Google Street View”, which is accurate and credits Google's service. The comparison to another guessing game is removed.
- **Breach 5v5 and Last Drop:** the teams are still called Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists. These are ordinary words, not trademarks, but they're closely tied to one famous shooter; I can rename them (e.g. Raiders / Defenders) if you like.
- **Gun Sim:** M1911, M1 Garand and M9 are US military designations, and .357 / .44 Magnum and .30-30 are cartridge names. These are generic, so I kept them. AK-47 became KR-47.
- **Four in a Row, Solitaire, Sudoku, Mahjong, Checkers, Chess:** generic game names, kept.
